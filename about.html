Our Story

A welcoming retreat. A little adventure. More time to enjoy the outdoors.

A Welcome from Dahlia

Welcome to Pacific Trails Resort! I’m Dahlia, the resort manager. My vision for Pacific Trails is simple: create a place where guests can slow down, feel welcome, and make room for adventure. A meaningful getaway can begin with something as small as a quiet walk or a morning spent listening to the ocean.

Our California coastal setting brings that vision to life. Comfortable private yurts and cooked-to-order breakfasts give guests a restful start before a day outside. Hiking trails, ocean kayaking, and guided bird-watching trips offer different ways to experience the landscape. Guests who prefer a gentler pace can enjoy the views or relax at the lodge.

Together, our team aims to make each stay comfortable and memorable. Whether you arrive with family, a friend, or time for yourself, we want you to leave feeling refreshed and more connected to the natural world.

What Matters to Us

  • Welcoming hospitality: Making guests feel comfortable and cared for.
  • Outdoor discovery: Encouraging exploration through hiking, kayaking, and bird watching.
  • Respect for nature: Staying on marked trails, keeping outdoor spaces clean, and observing wildlife quietly.
