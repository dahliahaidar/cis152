Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resort is surrounded by miles of hiking trails. Hike alone or in one of our guided hikes and take in the beautiful scenery.

Kayaking

Ocean kayaks are available for guest use.

Bird Watching

While anytime is a good time for bird watching at Pacific Trails, we offer guided bird watching trips at sunrise and sunset.
